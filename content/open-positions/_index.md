## Join our team

We are always on the lookout for motivated and curious minds to join our research group. Whether you're an undergraduate looking for your first hands-on experience in the lab, a graduate student eager to develop your thesis work, or a postdoctoral researcher aiming to deepen your expertise, we offer a stimulating and collaborative environment to explore bold scientific questions.

## Who we're looking for

We welcome applications from individuals with backgrounds in:

- Molecular biology, genomics, or developmental biology
- Bioinformatics, computational biology, or data science
- Any related discipline with a passion for discovery and innovation

We value diversity of thought and experience. You don't need to check every box: what matters most is your enthusiasm, creativity, and willingness to learn.
