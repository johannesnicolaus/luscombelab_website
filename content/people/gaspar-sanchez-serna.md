Gaspar is a biologist from Spain with a strong interest in evolutionary developmental biology (EvoDevo). He earned his PhD from the University of Barcelona, where he investigated how gene loss influences developmental evolution, using *Oikopleura dioica* and the FGF signaling pathway as a model system. In February 2025, he joined the GRSU at OIST to focus his research on the genomic basis of environmental adaptation in *O. dioica*.
