A computational biologist from Belgium, Nicolas developed the widely-used organelle assembler NOVOPlasty during his PhD and later researched disease-causing variants in university hospitals. Passionate about nature, he is now an interdisciplinary postdoc between the Marine Climate Change Unit of Prof. Ravasi and the Genomics and Regulatory Systems Unit.
