Anika is an undergraduate from IISER Pune, India, who joined the Luscombe Unit in June 2025 for a 10-month research stay. Her project focuses on investigating genome rearrangement rates across diverse lineages to understand the evolutionary forces driving genomic plasticity. She is particularly interested in developing computational methods to analyze large-scale genomic data and improve phylogenetic tree reconstruction. Anika is passionate about using bioinformatics to uncover patterns in genome evolution and contribute to our understanding of how genomes adapt and diversify.
