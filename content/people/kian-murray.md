Kian joined the unit as a PhD student in 2025. His research focuses on the ultrastructure and inflation dynamics of the larvacean cellulose-based house using cryo-EM and cryo-ET. Before coming to OIST, he earned his Bachelor’s degree in Zoology at the University of Manchester and researched bioluminescent proteins at Nagoya University.
